**Problem Title:** SIH 26033 – Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' earnings and increase consumer prices.

**Problem Creator's Organization:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

**Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## **1. Proposed Solution**

We propose a digital marketplace that directly connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, and bulk buyers.

- Farmers can sell their products directly without depending on middlemen.
- Consumers can purchase fresh agricultural products at affordable prices.
- Bulk buyers can place orders directly with farmers.
- The platform provides transportation and delivery support.
- AI technology predicts product demand and helps farmers decide what to grow.
- Smart route optimization reduces delivery time and transportation costs.

**Innovation and Uniqueness:**

The platform combines AI, digital marketing, and logistics to create a transparent marketplace that benefits both farmers and consumers.

## **2. Technical Approach**

**Technologies to be Used:**

- HTML, CSS and JavaScript for frontend development.
- Python for backend development.
- MySQL or MongoDB for database management.
- Machine Learning for demand forecasting.
- Google Maps API for route optimization.
- UPI and online payment gateways for transactions.

**Methodology and Implementation:**

1. Farmers register and upload their products.
2. Buyers search and select the required products.
3. The system connects buyers with nearby farmers.
4. AI predicts market demand and product requirements.
5. Orders are processed through the platform.
6. Logistics support arranges product delivery.
7. Online payment and order tracking are provided.

## **3. Feasibility and Viability**

**Feasibility of the Idea:**

- The platform can be developed using existing technologies.
- Smartphones and internet connections make it accessible.
- Digital payment systems make transactions easier.
- AI and GPS can improve market predictions and transportation.

**Potential Challenges and Risks:**

- Lack of technical knowledge among farmers.
- Poor internet connectivity in rural areas.
- Transportation and storage difficulties.
- Changes in agricultural product prices.
- Payment and security issues.

**Strategies to Overcome Challenges:**

- Develop a simple and user-friendly application.
- Provide local language support.
- Conduct digital awareness programs for farmers.
- Partner with transport companies and warehouses.
- Use secure payment gateways.
- Provide transparent pricing and customer support.

## **4. Impact and Benefits**

**Potential Impact on Target Audience:**

- Farmers receive better prices for their products.
- Consumers get fresh products at lower prices.
- Rural farmers gain access to wider markets.
- Bulk buyers can purchase products directly.
- The agricultural supply chain becomes more efficient.

**Benefits of the Solution:**

*Economic Benefits:*

- Reduces middlemen commissions.
- Increases farmers' income.
- Reduces consumer prices.

*Social Benefits:*

- Supports rural development.
- Encourages digital literacy.
- Creates employment opportunities.

*Environmental Benefits:*

- Reduces food wastage.
- Saves fuel through optimized routes.
- Reduces transportation-related pollution.
